Links now preview as what they actually point to. Pasting a JitaSpace link into Discord, Slack, WhatsApp, or Twitter used to show the same generic "JitaSpace — EVE Online companion app" blurb whatever the link was for. Every page now unfurls with its own title, description, and a generated preview card showing the entity's artwork and key facts — a ship's render with its group and category, a solar system's security status, region, and constellation, a station's system and owner, a character's portrait, corporation, and alliance, a war's two sides.
