Redesigned character pages to match the alliance, corporation and item pages: a summary header (age, corporations, current tenure, agent level, ISK efficiency) and tabs for an overview, biography, employment history, agent details and zKillboard statistics. The overview now shows the achievement score, ancestry, and the corporations a character leads or founded, and the employment history adds corporation counts and current, average and longest tenures. Agents show their type, division, start date, station, the dungeon and ship they sit in when in space, and their research fields. Character pages now load faster and fully render without JavaScript.
