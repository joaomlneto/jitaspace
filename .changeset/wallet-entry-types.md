The character wallet journal now has a Type column that names each transaction the way EVE does — "Brokers Fee", "Bounty Prizes", "Transaction Tax" — instead of leaving you to work it out from the description. Types that EVE explains in more detail show that explanation on hover, and the column filters by type, so you can narrow a busy journal down to just market fees or just bounties.
