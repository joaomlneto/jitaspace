The EVE theme now looks more like the EVE launcher: square corners, flat translucent panels over the wallpaper, neutral greys, buttons filled in the launcher's teal with dark text, borderless menus and dropdowns, see-through hover cards, and launcher-style tooltips, labels and scrollbars. The faction themes and the seasonal default theme pick up the square corners and dropdowns too. The WHPD theme is now the EVE theme on a plain black background. New "EVE (compact)" theme: the EVE look with tighter spacing and shorter controls, so more fits on screen; on touchscreens it keeps full-size controls. Coloured buttons and badges, security-status badges and links in running text are now easier to read in every EVE-based theme.
