Corporation pages and cards now show the corporation's own colours: a stripe of its colours along the top, a subtle wash of their hues behind its name, and a ticker badge in its main colour that blends slightly towards its secondary. The page also lists the colours as swatches, and shows the corporation logo square instead of cropping it to a circle. Corporations without colours keep a plain header.
