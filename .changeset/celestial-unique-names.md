Fixed the names of 19 moons and asteroid belts that have names of their own. For example, Kor-Azor Prime IV's moons now show as "Moon Griklaeum" rather than "Moon 1", and belts that were shown with the wrong number (such as "Asteroid Belt 5" for what the game calls "Asteroid Belt 3") now match the game.
