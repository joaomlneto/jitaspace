New Military Campaigns page: every campaign ESI lists — named, with its issuing faction and how close it came to its target — and each of its objectives with what it asks for, its progress towards its target and how many pilots took part. For your signed-in characters it adds which objectives they joined, whether they are still committed and what they contributed to each. Characters that have not granted the new campaign-activity permission get a one-click button to add it.
