Solar systems that nobody holds sovereignty over now show their star again. The sovereignty icon on solar system and killmail pages, and on every system and killmail card, falls back to the system's star when no alliance, corporation or faction owns the system — but that fallback was rendering the generic "unknown" placeholder instead of the star. High-sec, low-sec and wormhole systems now show the actual star.
