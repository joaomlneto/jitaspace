NPC and drone item pages have a new Combat tab: alpha and DPS by damage type, turret optimal, falloff and tracking, shield, armor and structure hit points with resistances and effective HP, speeds and ranges, and everything else it does: webs, scrams, neuts, target painters, jammers, sensor dampeners, weapon disruptors, remote repair, mining and salvaging. Everything is computed from the item's attributes; for drones these are base figures, before skills and bonuses.
