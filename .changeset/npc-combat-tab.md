NPC and drone item pages have a new Combat tab: alpha and DPS by damage type, turret optimal, falloff and tracking, shield, armor and structure hit points with resistances and effective HP, speeds and ranges, and everything else it does: webs, warp scramblers and disruptors, neuts and nosferatus, target painters, jammers, sensor dampeners, tracking, guidance and weapon disruptors, remote repair, fleet bonuses, mining and salvaging, each with its range, falloff, duration and strength. Everything is computed from the item's dogma attributes and effects; for drones these are base figures, before skills and bonuses.
