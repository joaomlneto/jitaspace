New look for the season: the default theme is now **Crimson Harvest**, a blood-red, ember-lit take on the EVE theme with the event's key art as its wallpaper. It will change with future in-game events.

The theme picker is also simpler: the refreshed EVE look (previously "EVE v2") is now the one "EVE" theme, and the Amarr, Caldari, Gallente and Minmatar themes are built on it, with their own colours and wallpapers. ORE, Sisters of EVE, Carbon and Photon join them as new EVE-based themes. The plain theme is now called "Minimal". If you had picked "EVE v2", you are now on the Default theme; pick "EVE" to get that look back.
