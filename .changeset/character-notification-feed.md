A notification feed in the header, like the in-game one: its icon shows how many unread notifications your logged-in characters have, and clicking it opens their combined notification history, newest first, with the portraits of the characters each one was sent to. A notice sent to several of your characters (a structure under attack, a war) is listed and counted once. Each notification says what it is about (the corporation you were invited to, the ship you lost, the two sides of a war, the structure and system under attack), and clicking one takes you there: the killmail, the corporation, the war, the biomassed member, the system. The old `/notifications` debug page has been removed.
