A notification feed in the header, like the in-game one: its icon shows how many unread notifications your logged-in characters have, and clicking it opens their combined notification history, newest first, with each character's portrait on their own notifications. Each notification says what it is about (the corporation you were invited to, the ship you lost, the two sides of a war, the structure and system under attack), and clicking one takes you there: the killmail, the corporation, the war, the biomassed member, the system. The old `/notifications` debug page has been removed.
