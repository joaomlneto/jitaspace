Added an Incursions page:

- **Status:** every active Sansha incursion, high-sec first, with its influence and a 72-hour influence chart, region, staging system and who holds it, whether the boss has spawned, when it started and the longest it can still last. Each system is listed under its site type (Vanguard, Assault, Headquarters), with its size and its stations (repair facilities marked). When no high-sec incursion is up, a countdown shows when the next one can spawn.
- **Timeline:** every incursion appearing, changing state and ending, most recent first, grouped by day.
- **Archive:** every incursion that has ended, back to 2015 with the history eve-incursions.de and EVE Ref recorded, in a sortable, filterable table with its region, staging system, start, end and duration.
- **Rats:** every Sansha incursion NPC in one sortable table: DPS and alpha by damage type, effective HP, shield and armor resistances, range, speed, signature and electronic warfare.
