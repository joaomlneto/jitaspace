Change-history filters and the Compare tool selection now persist in the URL. On the history pages, the tracked-collection chips and the "show unchanged builds" toggle stay in the address bar; on the Compare tool, the items you are comparing do too — so a filtered history view or a side-by-side comparison can be shared, bookmarked, and survives a refresh instead of resetting.
