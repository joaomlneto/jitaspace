Added a market quickbar, like the one in the game client. Star an item on its market page, right-click it in Market groups, or drag it onto the Quickbar tab to keep it at hand. Organise items into folders you can name and nest, then drag them around or use each row's menu to move, rename and delete. The sidebar's search box searches your quickbar too. Your quickbar is saved in this browser.
