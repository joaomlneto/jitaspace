Redesigned war pages. The header now sets the aggressor and the defender against each other, each with what they have destroyed, above a bar showing each side's share of the ISK destroyed, the war's status, its dates and its totals. Below it are Overview, Killmails and Allies tabs. Killmails are listed as compact, aligned rows, killboard style: EVE time, the ship lost, the victim, the final blow, the system and the kill's ISK value from zKillboard, marked by which side took the loss, with buttons to see the fitting or open the kill on zKillboard or EVE-Kill. They also load far faster on wars with many kills. The defender's info-window button now opens the defender instead of the aggressor.
