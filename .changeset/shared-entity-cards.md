Alliance, corporation and character cards now share one consistent design. On the home page, your corporations and alliances appear as compact one-line cards with their menu on the right. Hovering a character shows a compact card with their corporation and alliance, and the CSM 21 candidates page uses the same compact card for each candidate. Large alliances list their first 24 member corporations and count the rest.
