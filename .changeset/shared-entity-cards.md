Alliance, corporation, character and killmail cards now share one consistent design. On the home page, your corporations and alliances appear as compact one-line cards with their menu on the right. Hovering a character shows a compact card with their corporation and alliance. A war's killmails list as compact cards with the victim, ship and system, next to the lost ship's fitting. Large alliances list their first 24 member corporations and count the rest.
