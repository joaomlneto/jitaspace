Ships, items, solar systems, stations, planets, moons, stars and stargates keep showing their names and details now that EVE has retired its old universe API: JitaSpace reads them from CCP's new static-data service instead. Names of these now follow the language chosen in Settings without reloading. Station pages show the details from JitaSpace's own records, as EVE no longer serves live station data.
