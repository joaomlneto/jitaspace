Added Planetary Interaction pages. **Colonies** brings together every logged-in character's planets: when each extractor program ends, what your factories make, what is in storage, and how much of each commodity you produce per day, with charts, filters by character and status, and a map of each colony. **Customs Offices** lists your corporations' offices with their tax rates, access rules and reinforcement windows (Directors only). **Schematics** shows every planetary production schematic from P1 to P4, with inputs, outputs, cycle times and what uses each product.
